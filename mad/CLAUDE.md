# mad — Domain Router (ONE domain, two sub-areas)

**Mad LLC.** This is the **one relaxed boundary** in the vault: `customs/` and
`armory/` are **sub-areas of a single domain**, not separate domains — because they
share the LLC, the brand palette, and the `mad-custom-tx` repo. But the split must stay
**visible**.

## Sub-areas
| Sub-area | Path | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Customs | `customs/` | Mad Customs work + knowledge |
| Armory | `armory/` | Mad Armory work + knowledge — **regulated** (see wall) |
| Shared | `_shared/` | ONLY genuinely cross-cutting Mad things |

## What `_shared/` may hold
Brand, LLC entity, shared infrastructure, and the shared `mad-custom-tx` repo context.
**That's it.** `_shared/` is internal to `mad` — it is not a bridge to other domains.

## ⚠️ COMPLIANCE WALL (critical)
Armory's **FFL / Class-02-SOT / NFA** regulatory knowledge is a **different legal
surface**. Therefore:
- It must **NOT** leak into `customs/`.
- It must **NOT** live in `_shared/`.
- It lives **only** under `armory/` (e.g. `armory/knowledge/`).
- When in doubt whether something is regulated, treat it as regulated and keep it in
  `armory/`.

This wall is stricter than the normal domain wall. Customs and armory may share brand
and LLC via `_shared/`, but regulatory knowledge never crosses.

## Isolation declaration (vs. other top-level domains)
- `mad` references **only** Mad. It does **not** link to `fugro`, `zus`, or `personal`.

- `armory/{knowledge,skills,now.md}`  ← regulated; compliance wall applies.
