# zus — Domain Router (ONE domain, two sub-areas)

The **zus-legacy** project. Like `mad`, this is **one domain with sub-areas** — a
**web platform** and an **Android companion app** that serve the same ZUS clan but live
in two separate repos. The split must stay **visible**.

## Sub-areas
| Sub-area | Path | Repo | Scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| Web | `web/` | `nextjs-boilerplate` (zuslegacy.com) | The Next.js clan-intelligence platform |
| App | `app/` | `zus-legacy-companion` (Android) | The Android companion app |
| Shared | `_shared/` | — | ONLY the cross-cutting data contract (see below) |

## What `_shared/` may hold
The genuinely cross-cutting ZUS **data contract** that both web and app reason about:
the game-data decode/protocol, the title id→name map, and the clan-identity / clan
filtering rules. **That's it.** `_shared/` is internal to `zus` — it is not a bridge to
other domains.

## Isolation declaration (vs. other top-level domains)
- `zus` references **only** zus. It does **not** link to `fugro`, `mad`, or `personal`.
  If something seems shared across top-level domains, **stop and ask** — never bridge.
